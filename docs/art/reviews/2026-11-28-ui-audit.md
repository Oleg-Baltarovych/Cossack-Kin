Input: `reports/screens/2026-11-28/ui-audit/*` (74 shots, 390×844@3, 1300 / 2100). Reference: `docs/art/ART-BIBLE.md` §4, §9, `.claude/rules/ui.md`. Companion: `2026-11-28-ux-audit.md` (ui-reviewer).

Colour inventory of the UI styles:
- `wood 0–3`, `ink 0`, `plaster 3–6`, `gold 2–5`, `thatch 5–6` and `stone 1–2` cover almost everything.
- `crimson` appears once.
- There is no `border-image` in `src/ui`. Art bible §9 («UI-рамки — дерево й полотно з вишиваним орнаментом, 9-slice») is not built yet.

## 1. Verdict: ⚠ fix first (UI skin rework)
The UI works and reads, but it is a programmer's skin.
- **One flat brown.** `wood 0–2` in three steps about 15 % apart, with 1-px lines and no material.
- **Out of step with the world.** The world is painted with light, texture and hue-shifted ramps; the UI has none of that.
- **Narrow accents.** Only yellow, pale ochre, green and a cold slate grey. Crimson (корогва, вишивка) is missing.
- **One crafted surface.** The raid-map parchment frame (06) shows how far the rest is from the bible.

## 2. Problems

### High
1. **No material on any layer.**
   - HUD, tab bar, sheet, rows, cards and modals are solid fills with 1-px lines.
   - There is no grain, bevel or ornament.
   - Evidence: 02-farm-mid-full-night, 09-more-treasury-day, 06-raid-map-day.
2. **No surface hierarchy.**
   - Frame, panel, card and control all sit within `wood 0` #3b2419 → `wood 2` #774a28.
   - Hero and relic cards use the same recipe as the unselected picker buttons: a card looks tappable and a button looks like a card.
   - The quest strip, the picker and the «Походи» header are three bands of nearly one brown.
   - Evidence: 05-sich-raids-full-night, 08-culture-full-heroes-day, 03-quest-more-full-day.
3. **Larger text clips price buttons.**
   - From «Город» down, the coin and the end of the number are cut («1,02млр,»).
   - This is a layout bug.
   - Evidence: 10-large-text-farm-full-day.

### Medium
4. **Accents carry no stable meaning.**
   - `gold 4` stands for buy, claim, the selected picker, the active tab, the open node and progress at once.
   - `thatch 5` ochre is used both for headings and levels and for the «Бракує …» warning and timers.
   - Evidence: 02-farm-mid-full-night, 05-sich-idle-full-bottom-day.
5. **Locked/disabled uses `stone 2` slate #555867.**
   - It is the only cold blue-grey in a warm UI, laid as large slabs.
   - The disabled «Вирушити» has a dashed web border.
   - Evidence: 08-culture-tree-customs-day, 05-sich-idle-full-bottom-day, 09-more-treasury-day, 06-raid-map-far-card-night.
6. **The UI ignores the world it frames.**
   - Day and night look the same.
   - The HUD (about 27 % of the height) and the tab bar are opaque slabs with no lip, so the scene looks letterboxed.
   - The brown modal scrim turns dusk into mud.
   - Evidence: 01-fresh-collapsed-night, 02-farm-mid-half-day, 07-raid-chest-night, 12-story-beat-messenger-night.
7. **Modals are flat boxes with web touches.**
   - The only ornament is a 1-px orange top line.
   - The white ring on the primary button reads as a browser focus ring.
   - The title has no plaque.
   - The reward inset uses the list-card fill.
   - Evidence: 01-fresh-welcome-modal-day, 07-return-raids-day.

### Low
8. **Portrait plinths.**
   - A flat square with a 1-px border; the figure has no ground.
   - The unknown-relic tiles sit on a cold lavender plate.
9. **Text tiers are too close.**
   - Names and descriptions look alike.
   - Section headings have no rule.
   - «Ще» ends in a large empty field.
10. **Tier banner plaque** has low contrast under the scrim. It should share the new plaque recipe.

### Keep
- the raid-map parchment and studs;
- the gold primary button with its 2-px base;
- the icon set and pixel discipline;
- the portraits;
- green for gains;
- the tree's quarter-column lines.

## 3. Directions

### A. «Різьблене дерево й рушник» (recommended)
The UI as khata furniture: a carved oak lintel, a plank board and a linen towel with cross-stitch.

**Layers**
- **Frame** (HUD, sheet top, modal border):
  - `wood 0` with a 2-px bevel lit from the top-left (`wood 3` / `ink 1`);
  - a 2-px «зубці» notch band as the lip between UI and sky.
- **Panel:** `wood 1` with sparse vertical grain (a 1-px `wood 0` streak every 7–11 px).
- **Card:** sunken (inner `ink 1` on top/left, `wood 2` on bottom/right).
- **Control:** raised.
  - primary: `gold 4` / `gold 2` base;
  - secondary: `wood 3` with a bevel;
  - selected: a 2-px `crimson 3` frame;
  - disabled: flat `wood 1`, `plaster 2` text and a lock.
- **Ornament:** a рушник strip, 6 px tall (ромб / «ружа» stitches in `crimson 3` / `crimson 1` on `plaster 4` linen). Only three places: the modal title band, the section rule and the centre of the HUD's lower edge.

**Accents**
- crimson: selection and culture identity;
- gold: buy or claim now;
- ochre: headings and levels only;
- green: gains;
- `crimson 5` with an icon: a shortage.

**Cost and risk**
- Art: one small `ui-frame` sheet (about 4 nine-slices, 16–24 px), separate from the `ui` atlas.
- Risk: kitsch if the ornament spreads beyond three places, and noise if the grain is above 1 step of contrast.

### B. «Універсал і печатка»
Parchment everywhere, a wax seal for primary actions and a ribbon for selection.
- Risk is high for the sheet: a light surface glares at night, kills the dusk and needs every text colour re-checked.
- Use it only for document windows: story beats, relic lore, the return window, the raid map.

### C. «Кахлі»
Terracotta and cobalt stove tiles.
- Too colourful; it fights the world, and cobalt on white drifts to Delft.
- Use it only as tiny accents (portrait plinths, branch icon frames).

### Recommendation
A, with B's parchment for document windows and C only for plinths. A uses the world's own wood and light direction, stays dark at night, gives crimson a job, and needs one small new sheet.

## 4. Quick wins (CSS + palette only)
All sizes in whole `--px` steps; no colours outside `master.json`.

1. **Kill the slate.**
   - Locked, unaffordable and unknown-relic surfaces get a flat `wood 1` fill, a 1-px `ink 1` border, `plaster 2` text and the price in `thatch 3`.
   - Replace the dashed disabled border with the same recipe.
2. **Bevel by `box-shadow`, with three surface roles:**
   - raised controls: `wood 3` on top/left, `ink 1` on bottom/right;
   - sunken cards: the reverse;
   - panel: flat.
3. **Lip between UI and sky.**
   - Under the HUD: 1-px `wood 3`, then 1-px `wood 0`, then a 2-px `ink 0` line.
   - On the sheet top: a 1-px `wood 3` rim.
4. **Accent semantics.**
   - Selection (tab, picker, tree node) gets a 2-px `crimson 3` frame.
   - Gold is reserved for buy/claim.
   - «Бракує …» goes to `crimson 5` with an icon.
   - The selected picker button: `wood 3` raised with a crimson frame instead of the pale fill.
5. **Section headings with a stitched rule.**
   - Heading in `thatch 5`, then a 1-px `crimson 3` running stitch at 2·`--px` steps.
   - Check for seams at 412@3.5; if they show, use a solid `wood 2` rule.
6. **Modals.**
   - Drop the white ring on the primary button.
   - Replace the orange top line with a title band: a `wood 0` plaque, with 2-px `crimson 3` and 1-px `gold 2` under it.
   - Give the reward inset the sunken-card recipe.
7. **Night scrim.** At dusk and night use a cool `ink 1` / `cloth 0` scrim instead of the brown one. This is UI only.
8. **Text tiers.**
   - names `plaster 6`, descriptions `plaster 3`, meta `plaster 2`;
   - green only on the delta, not on whole effect sentences.
9. **Portrait plinth:**
   - a 4–6-px `earth 2` ground strip;
   - a 1-px `wood 4` rim on top/left and an `ink 1` rim on bottom/right.
10. **Fix the larger-text price clipping** first. It is a layout bug.

Order: 1, 4, 2, 3, 6 give the most change for the least risk. Run the full `@screens` matrix after them: the Linux baselines will move.
