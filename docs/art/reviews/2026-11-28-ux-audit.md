# UX audit of the DOM UI — 2026-11-28 (ui-reviewer)

Input: `reports/screens/2026-11-28/ui-audit/` (74 PNGs, 390×844@3, day/night). The reviewer opened 10 of them; findings on the raid map, chest, heroes, English and story screens are inferred from SPEC §6.1/§10 and must be confirmed on the screens.

## Verdict
The core loop is readable and the structure is sound: a two-line HUD + goal strip, one ×1/×10/×25/Max picker, yellow «can buy» vs grey «can't», «now → after» lines. The weak spots are the shell and the information design:
- the settings hold only the language switch;
- the HUD is static, with no breakdown;
- the HUD is tall;
- nothing says «best buy / why locked / what next» beyond the goal strip;
- larger text breaks the price buttons and the HUD.

## High
- **H1 Settings = language only** (09-more-settings-day).
  - SPEC §6.8/§10.5 want volumes, «менше руху», «більший текст», vibration, save export/import, reset, credits.
  - Sound is M7, accessibility M9. **Export/import and reset are in no milestone.**
  - The «менше руху» and «більший текст» toggles are cheap and could ship early.
- **H2 Larger text breaks price buttons and the HUD** (10-large-text-farm-full-day).
  - Price buttons clip the coin and the suffix, and the HUD numbers collide.
  - Fix: price column `min-content`, price under the name at large text, a wrapping HUD.
- **H3 HUD not tappable** (all screens).
  - Fix: §10.3 breakdown of the sources (M4-14).
  - Also explain «Харч» and «Оборона», and add a visible tap affordance.
- **H4 HUD height and density.**
  - The HUD is 4 rows, the wind sits alone on a row, and its height differs between screens.
  - Fix: 2 rows, stat chips, wind as an icon only, a fixed height.
- **H5 No «best buy» or delta cue** (02-farm-mid-full).
  - Three yellow buttons, and nothing says which is worth more.
  - Fix: a best-value badge from a core selector, and the gain as one number «+1,3/с».
  - In no milestone.
- **H6 Return window has no «what next».**
  - Fix: a «Що далі» line with a CTA to Січ when a raid waits.
  - Fix: the cap as a plain fact, not a warning.

## Medium
- **M1 Tab bar.**
  - The ▼ cell is unlabeled.
  - Inactive tabs have low contrast.
  - 5 tabs + the arrow will not fit at 390 once «Край» comes in M5.
  - Fix: a drag-handle pill and icon+label tabs.
- **M2 Red dots show only when the sheet is collapsed.**
  - A returned raid on Січ is invisible from Хутір.
  - Show the raid dot always (needs a SPEC edit).
- **M3 One yellow for every action.**
  - Claim and buy share the yellow, and the reward text uses the income green.
  - One colour per meaning; add a progress bar to the strip.
- **M4 The half sheet shows little.**
  - On Січ the «Забрати» is cut off.
  - Fix: dock the picker as a chip, raid cards first.
- **M5 Rows wrap to 4 lines.**
  - The arrow is orphaned at the line end in «+5,04 тис./с → …».
  - Fix: show the delta only, `nowrap` on the pair, «до ×2: 18 рів.».
- **M6 «Бракує дукатів» ×5 with no distance.**
  - Fix: time to afford («ще 2 хв») from a selector.
- **M7 Culture node card.**
  - The card covers the tree, and the disabled «Вивчити» is ambiguous.
  - The selected node is marked only by a thin border.
  - Fix: a full-width button with the reason on it; auto-scroll the node above the card.
- **M8 «Ще» is a long mixed list.**
  - Settings sit under the relics.
  - Fix: a menu with pages, or a segment control; a gear in the HUD (SPEC §6.1 draws one).
- **M9 Onboarding.**
  - No pointer to the first buy and no «тапай по селу» hint.
  - Cheap highlight now; full arrows M9.
- **M10 «Too dear» looks the same as «locked by a condition».**
  - Fix: lock icon + condition on locked rows (SPEC wants it).

## Low
- **L1 Secondary text contrast** looks under 4.5:1 (taupe on brown). Measure it and use a token.
- **L2 Number suffix spacing** differs: the HUD «48,5 тис.» vs the buttons «12,6тис.».
- **L3 «Культура»** shows at game start with nothing to do. Lock it with a reason or hide it.
- **L4 Raid card buttons** are uneven («Ще раз» cut off). Use a full-width two-button row.
- **L5 Story modals** are fine. Keep them short.

## Structural ideas for the refresh
1. HUD: 2 rows of tappable chips, plus a gear.
2. Bottom bar: a handle pill and 5 icon+label tabs, with dots on any tab that has something to collect.
3. The picker as a compact chip in the sheet header.
4. Rows: a building thumbnail, a one-line delta, a «best buy» mark, time to afford.
5. Culture: branch sub-tabs, with the card as an overlay that never covers the chosen node.
6. «Ще»: a menu with pages.
7. Return window: a «Що далі» block.
8. Keep actions in the lower half; the HUD only informs.

## Not in any milestone yet
- save export/import and reset;
- best-buy and time-to-afford cues;
- always-on raid dot;
- large-text layout of rows and HUD;
- HUD gear;
- locking «Культура» early.
